Crayon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Crayon is an AI chat assistant at crayon-web.onrender.com and its Android app. This page explains what happens to your data.

What you type

Your messages are sent to our server and passed to AI model providers (Google Gemini, and free models served through OpenRouter) so they can write a reply.

Memory

So Crayon can continue a conversation later, your recent messages are saved in our database (Postgres hosted on Neon). Every message is encrypted before it is stored. Your saved chats are tied to an anonymous id kept in a signed cookie on your device - or to your Google account if you sign in. Only you ever see your memory: Crayon never shows one visitor another visitor's chats. Saved messages are kept for at most 90 days, and the Forget me button at the top of the page deletes everything saved about you instantly. The "New chat" button starts a fresh conversation without deleting older memory.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose "Sign in", Google tells us your name, email and profile picture so Crayon can show your profile and attach your memory to your account instead of just this device. We store only those three fields. Signing in is optional - you can chat without it - and you can sign out at any time from your avatar. Chats you had as a guest on the same device are merged into your account when you first sign in.

Shared chats

Nothing is public unless you press Share. That creates a read-only link containing a snapshot of your current chat. Anyone you give the link to can read that snapshot. Shared pages are marked noindex so search engines are asked not to list them. Deleting your memory with "Forget me" does not remove share links you already made.

Files and code

Files you attach and any code Crayon runs stay in your browser. Short excerpts of an attached file may be sent to the AI model so it can answer questions about it. Files are never stored on our server.

Image generation

When you ask Crayon to make an image, the image prompt is sent to Pollinations (a free image service) which creates the picture. Do not put private information in image prompts.

Discord bot (optional)

If you chat with Crayon on Discord, the same rules as this page apply: messages go to the AI providers to write replies, and your recent conversation is saved encrypted so the bot remembers you. Tell the bot "forget me" any time to wipe what it stores about you.

Voice

Voice input and spoken replies run in your browser using its built-in speech features. On most browsers the audio is processed by the browser maker (for example Google in Chrome) under their own policies; Crayon does not receive or store your audio.

Web search and page reading

When Crayon searches or opens a web page for you, the search words or page address are sent from our server to a search engine or that website.

Gmail (optional)

Only if you choose "Connect Gmail", you sign in with Google and grant access to your own mailbox (the gmail.modify permission). Crayon uses it only to do what you ask in chat, such as search, read, label, draft or send, and every change needs your approval first. Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted in our database, linked only to your account, and you can remove it any time with "Disconnect", which revokes the token at Google and deletes it from our database. Email content is sent to the AI model only to answer your request. Crayon's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for ads, or let humans read it.

Technical data

Our host logs normal request information such as IP address and time. We use your IP address only for abuse limits (rate limiting). A signed cookie keeps your anonymous identity so memory works; separate cookies keep you signed in if you use Google sign-in or connect Gmail. We keep anonymous, aggregated usage counts (messages per day, which features are used) to run the service.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the providers named above, and only as needed to give you an answer.

Children

Crayon is not intended for children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can stop using Crayon at any time, press "Forget me" to delete your saved memory, sign out, disconnect Gmail, and clear your browser data. To ask about your data, email teamalmostinstinct@gmail.com.

Crayon is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important information.
